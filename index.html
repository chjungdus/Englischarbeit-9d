Teil 1

Der ultimative Spickzettel

Alle 5 Zeitformen auf einen Blick. Formel + Signalwörter + Beispiel lern auswendig!

Zeitform Bildung Signalwörter Beispielsatz
Simple Past V2 / verb + ed yesterday · ago · last night / week / year · in 1990 · then · when She called him yesterday.
Past Continuous was / were + verb+ing while · when · at that moment · at 6 o'clock (yesterday) He was sleeping when she called.
Past Perfect Simple
(Active)		 had + V3 already · just · yet · before · after · by the time · never … before They had left before I arrived.
Past Perfect Simple
(Passive)		 had + been + V3 before · already · by the time
(Fokus: Objekt, nicht Täter)		 The letter had been sent before noon.
Past Perfect
Continuous		 had + been + verb+ing for · since · how long · all day / week
(Betonung der Dauer!)		 She had been waiting for an hour when the bus arrived.
⚡

Unterbrechung / parallel

Past Continuous (lange Handlung) + Simple Past (kurze Unterbrechung). Schlüssel: while / when

⏮

Vorvergangenheit

Handlung A passiert VOR Handlung B in der Vergangenheit → Past Perfect Simple (Active)

🔒

Täter unwichtig

Objekt im Fokus, kein Täter genannt → Past Perfect Passive: had + been + V3

⏱

Dauer bis zur Vergangenheit

Wie lange lief etwas BIS zu einem Punkt? → Past Perfect Continuous. Schlüssel: for / since

🎯

Stolperfalle: PP Simple vs. Continuous

Beide nutzen had — Continuous betont die DAUER, Simple betont das ERGEBNIS / den Abschluss

🧩

Passiv erkennen

Kein Täter / Objekt als Subjekt / "worden" im Deutschen → Passiv wählen: had been + V3

Schnell-Referenz — Bildungsformeln

walk → walked

stop → stopped

Past Perfect Simple
